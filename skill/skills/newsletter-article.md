# Skill: newsletter / email and long-form article

Structure after stanford-oval/storm (MIT: a lead that stands alone, one section per question, citations, sources) and the strict-format idea of anthropics/skills internal-comms (Apache-2.0), in our own words.

Newsletter (`--for newsletter`)
- First lines of post.md: `subject: ...` (about 40 characters, clear before clever, what is inside) and `preview: ...` (about 90 characters, adds to the subject, never repeats it). Then `# Title`.
- One mail, one job. Greeting in the user's voice, the most useful thing first, short paragraphs of one to three sentences, a `##` heading per part, one clear next step at the end, signed like a person.
- From research: `[S1]` marks are fine, and the mail ends with `## 출처` / `## Sources`.

Long-form article (`--for article`)
1. Outline twice: first from what you know (only headings), then fix it with the fact sheet: one `##` section per question from questions.md that the sources answer.
2. Lead (first paragraphs, no heading): what it is, why it matters now, the key points, including the critical view. At most four short paragraphs.
3. Each section only from its facts, `[S1]` after the sentence it supports. No separate "introduction" or "conclusion" sections that repeat.
4. Read it once more only for repeats; delete repeated information, nothing else.
5. End with `## 출처` / `## Sources`: one line per source used, title, site, date, link.
6. `keyword: ...` for search; build, check, show.
