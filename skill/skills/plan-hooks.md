# Skill: plan + hooks (angle, title, first line)

Title and hook shapes adapted from coreyhaines31/marketingskills (MIT); the scoring is ours.

1. After `start`, run `CW plan <run>`. It writes `plan.md` with: keyword (the search words), angle, for who, a titles list and a hooks list per platform, each with shapes to fill.
2. Write 5 to 10 titles and 3 to 5 first lines per platform. A shape is only a shape: fill it with a specific fact, number or scene from the source. For each line ask: can the reader picture it? Can it be checked? Could anyone else write this exact line? If yes, rewrite it.
3. `CW plan <run> --score` ranks them: length for the platform, search words near the front (blog), numbers only from the source, no AI-sounding or overclaiming words. Pick the best, use them in post.md.

Platform notes
- Naver blog: the title starts with the search words, then why to click (what you get, a real number). About 12 to 40 characters. Put `keyword: ...` in post.md; the words once in the first paragraph and in one heading. Short paragraphs, a heading every few paragraphs, a picture between paragraphs.
- Threads: the first line shows in the feed; the point goes there (about 60 characters). One idea per post, the best picture in the first post, one topic tag.
- X: the topic in the first few words, a real number, about 140 Korean letters a post (weighted 280).
- Instagram: slide 1 is the hook, one point per slide, the last slide says what to do. The first caption line (about 60 characters) is all most people see.
- LinkedIn: the first two lines before "see more" carry the point; a story with a work lesson; links in the comment, not the post.
- Newsletter: the subject is clear before clever (about 40 characters); the preview adds to it, never repeats it.
