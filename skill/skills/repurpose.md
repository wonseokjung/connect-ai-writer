# Skill: repurpose (one source to everything)

1. Pull 5 to 10 pieces out of the source first: the one result, the numbers, the best quotes, the steps, a story moment, the critical point. Each platform gets a different piece up front; do not paste the same text everywhere.
2. `CW start <source> --for all,newsletter` (or pick: threads,naver,x,linkedin,instagram,newsletter,article). One brief, one post.md per platform. Then `CW brief <run>` once: the same reader and next action for every platform. Write each in its own shape (write-platforms.md, newsletter-article.md).
3. Video of the post: `CW motion <run> --from threads` (skills/visuals.md).
4. A children's version: `CW story "<idea from the source>" --age 7` when the topic fits a child (space, animals, a small brave act). Never put real people in it.
5. Build once, check once, show `index.html`. Posting: drafts with `post`, or `handoff/writer-package.json` to connect-ai-marketer.
